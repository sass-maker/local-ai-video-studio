# Design Direction

Status: owner-approved `optical-printer-bench` overhaul direction.

## Direction contract

**THESIS:** One source becomes three inspectable film studies under a shared
probe. Refuse the prompt-first AI dashboard and the conventional editor whose
timeline overwhelms the comparison.

**OWN-WORLD:** Daylight inspection stock, ink-black machinery, restrained teal
and ochre treatments, square-cornered media wells, specimen-like parameter
notes, hairline rules, and compact physical controls.

**STORY:** Import one local clip, state the intent, inspect exactly what differs,
move all takes with one playhead, choose with or without labels, and export a
reproducible result.

**FIRST VIEWPORT:** A narrow source inspector sits left; three equal vertical
studies form one contact sheet; a prominent shared-probe difference strip spans
beneath them; graph-plan specimens and blind/export actions close the workspace.

**FORM:** Grounded direction 5, staged as a twinned/multi-variant probe from
seed `d812f099`; owner selected `optical-printer-bench` with the
`difference-strip` composition.

## Principles

- Media is the largest object. Controls, metadata, and graph detail support it
  rather than competing with it.
- Variants are equal-width studies, never nested cards. Their labels live in a
  paper data strip attached directly to each media well.
- The shared probe/playhead is the only continuous accent across variants. It
  uses fired-clay orange; local-ready and successful states use oxidized teal.
- The source bench stays visually quieter and narrower than the comparison
  field. Planning is explicit and inspectable, not conversational theater.
- Approximation, degraded, and failure states are written plainly beside the
  affected output. Color never carries state alone.

## Provisional tokens

- Canvas: `#E8E5DC`; secondary stock: `#D5D0C4`; study stock: `#F2EFE5`.
- Ink: `#1F2628`; muted ink: `#596062`; hairline: `#8B887F`.
- Probe/accent: `#C77957`; ready/success: `#4E8F87`; warning: `#B77A37`.
- Typography: San Francisco system faces for controls and reading; monospaced
  system digits only for timecode, dimensions, hashes, and measured parameters.
- Corners: 4–8 points for controls and bounded wells; no pill-shaped primary
  actions, decorative glass, glow, gradients, or soft floating cards.
- Spacing follows an 8-point base with compact 4-point metadata gaps and
  24–32-point separation between operational regions.

## Appearance

- Dark is the first-launch default for low-light editing; System and Light
  remain available from the persistent appearance control.
- Dark canvas: grading-room black `#050606`; bench: `#090B0B`; raised
  paper/study surfaces: `#0E1111` and `#070909`. Separation comes from
  graphite hairlines and hierarchy, not broad grey panels.
- Dark ink is warm silver `#E8E5DB`, muted ink is `#ADAFAB`, and hairlines are
  restrained graphite `#595C59`.
- Fired-clay probe, oxidized-teal success, and ochre warning become brighter in
  dark mode without turning neon. Media wells stay true black so footage,
  overlays, and fallback states remain visually authoritative.
- Dark mode is composed through semantic roles rather than mechanically
  inverting light values; labels and icons never rely on color alone.

## Layout and adaptation

- At wide windows, source/intent occupies about 22% and the three-study field
  owns the remainder.
- At medium widths, source/intent collapses into an inspector and studies remain
  horizontally scrollable rather than shrinking below useful review size.
- At narrow widths, studies stack one at a time with a persistent shared probe
  strip; variant switching preserves playhead time.
- Full keyboard traversal, visible focus, VoiceOver labels, reduced motion, and
  sufficient contrast are mandatory. Motion is limited to the playhead/probe,
  render progress, and direct state transitions.

## References and anti-reference

Transferable lessons come from DaVinci Resolve's comparison/timeline hierarchy,
Final Cut Pro's media-led organization, and Ableton Live's instrument-state
clarity. Do not copy their branding, assets, shortcuts, or proprietary visual
language. The anti-reference is the generic dark AI prompt app made from glowing
rounded cards: it obscures media, graph truth, and local render state.
